# Sales Performance & Business Analytics Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project showcases an interactive Microsoft Excel dashboard built to
explore business revenue, sales team performance, advertising channel
contribution, and training-related metrics.

The dashboard brings multiple business indicators into one view to
support performance monitoring and data-driven decision-making.

## Dashboard Highlights

-   **Revenue Overview:** Displays total earnings, monthly revenue
    trends, highest revenue, average revenue, and lowest revenue.
-   **Sales Team Performance:** Compares sales results across team
    members and highlights top-performing teams.
-   **Advertising Channel Analysis:** Compares revenue attributed to
    YouTube, Google Ads, WhatsApp, company website, Facebook, and
    television advertising.
-   **Consultant Performance:** Ranks consultants by sales revenue and
    highlights top contributors.
-   **Training Revenue Analysis:** Examines training model fees and
    training-level fees across sales teams.
-   **Paid and Unpaid Calls:** Summarizes paid versus unpaid calls and
    shows call-duration metrics.
-   **Training Enrollment:** Displays enrolled courses and average
    enrollment indicators.
-   **Interactive Filters:** Allows users to filter the dashboard by
    month and sales team.

## Tools & Techniques

-   Microsoft Excel
-   Pivot Tables and Pivot Charts
-   KPI tracking
-   Data analysis and visualization
-   Comparative performance analysis

## Business Value

The dashboard helps users review revenue patterns, compare sales team
performance, understand advertising channel contribution, and monitor
training-related indicators in a consolidated view.

## How to Use

1.  Download the Excel workbook from this repository.
2.  Open it in Microsoft Excel.
3.  Use the month and sales filters to explore the available metrics.
4.  Review the charts and KPIs to compare revenue, sales performance,
    advertising channels, and training metrics.
